📖 Understanding AQI & Health

🟢 0 - 50: Good

Minimal impact; ideal air quality for all outdoor sports and cardio.

🟡 51 - 100: Satisfactory

Minor breathing discomfort to highly sensitive individuals.

🟠 101 - 200: Moderate

Breathing discomfort to people with asthma and heart disease.

🔴 201 - 300: Poor (GRAP-I)

Breathing discomfort on prolonged exposure; wear an N95 mask outdoors.

🟣 301 - 400: Very Poor (GRAP-II)

Respiratory illness on prolonged exposure; avoid morning cardio.

🟤 401 - 500+: Severe / Severe+ (GRAP-III & IV)

Affects healthy people and seriously impacts those with existing diseases. Stay indoors with purifiers.

PureAir® Citizen App

A simple everyday app to check your local air quality, know what is causing the pollution, and plan outdoor activities safely.

  • Live AQI score and main reason for pollution
  • Simple breakdown of dust, gases, and weather
  • 1 to 3 day hourly air quality forecasts
  • Best times of day for outdoor walks and workouts
  • Report smoke and garbage burning with photos
  • Live city map showing wind direction and smoke spread

PureAir® Command Center

A control dashboard for city officials to spot pollution hotspots, send inspection teams, and test anti-pollution rules.

  • Live ranking of the most polluted areas in the city
  • Test rules before enforcing (Odd-Even, truck bans, mist guns)
  • Citizen smoke and fire reports with photo proof
  • Quick one-click dispatch for field inspection teams
  • Clear breakdown of pollution sources (traffic, factories, dust, fires)
  • Real-time AI accuracy tracker and health monitor

Satellite Active Fires & Crop Residue Burning

Near Real-Time Satellites

Thermal anomaly coordinates, Fire Radiative Power (FRP), and detection confidence from VIIRS (375m) and MODIS (1km) satellites over Punjab, Haryana, and Western UP coupled to agricultural harvest calendars.

Fire Lat/Lon Coordinates Fire Radiative Power (MW) Satellite Overpass (VIIRS / MODIS) Confidence Score (%) Transboundary Inflow (µg/m³)

Altitude Elevation (meters ASL) Terrain Slope & Ridge Profiles Topographic Barrier Decay

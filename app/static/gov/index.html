🔬 How PureAir® Calculates Atmospheric Predictions & Attributions

STAGE 1
Sensor Ingestion

Streams live pollutant readings from 40+ CPCB CAAQMS stations, Open-Meteo weather parameters (temperature, humidity, wind vectors), and NASA FIRMS satellite fire counts.

STAGE 2
Dynamic Plume Flow & Transport

Maps directed connectivity between hexagons based on 3D wind velocity, terrain elevation barriers (Aravalli Ridge), and atmospheric thermal inversion mixing depth.

STAGE 3
AI Spatiotemporal Forecasting

Spatial graph convolutions track pollutant movement downwind while temporal neural networks predict 1-to-72 hour AQI trends with 90% confidence uncertainty envelopes.

STAGE 4
Source Causality Attribution

Compares current pollution against a pristine clean-air baseline (15 µg/m³) using game-theoretic sensitivity to apportion percentage blame across all 7 source categories.

STAGE 5
Policy Impact Simulator

Simulates real-world policy actions (e.g. 50% traffic cut, 90% construction dust reduction) and calculates forward graph response to forecast expected AQI improvements.
